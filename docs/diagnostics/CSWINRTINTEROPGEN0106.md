# CSWINRTINTEROPGEN0106: Transitive generic discovery limit exceeded

The interop generator follows at most 1024 distinct transitive generic type or method instantiations eligible for member or body scanning in each discovery pass. Type members and generic method bodies share this budget. This prevents branching return types such as `Node<A<T>>` and `Node<B<T>>`, or branching generic helper calls, from creating exponentially many work items even when each signature stays below the [complexity limit](CSWINRTINTEROPGEN0105.md) and the traversal stays below the [depth limit](CSWINRTINTEROPGEN0104.md). Types with no eligible members, such as framework generics excluded by the marshalling mode that have no static initializer, do not consume this budget. Unresolved methods, methods without IL bodies, and transitive callees skipped by the marshalling policy do not consume it either. Their referenced signatures and method arguments are still considered for marshalling.

Ordinary discovery runs first with its existing 1024 allowance and first-visit ordering. Generic dispatch observations and shorter-depth sightings do not spend that allowance or enqueue additional primary work. Once primary discovery finishes, an isolated supplemental enumeration expands interface implementations, virtual overrides, and depth-limited contexts using a separate 1024 allowance. Every transitive type or ordinary callee reached from this additional work is charged to the supplemental allowance, never to the primary allowance. The supplemental phase has its own worklist and visited state, and its results are added to the primary results rather than replacing them.

Non-generic default interface bodies closed by observed generic receivers use the same supplemental allowance. They run after the existing supplemental queue drains, and each selected body context reserves an entry just like a generic method body. Their ordinary helpers and newly discovered receiver contexts remain in that traversal; subsequent default-body batches do not reset the counter. Reaching the limit before those batches can therefore leave default-body-derived marshalling unavailable without displacing earlier primary or module-local supplemental work.

Additional constrained static interface calls run after the instance-default fixpoint and share its remaining allowance. Non-generic static slots use type-only body contexts; generic slots retain their actual method arguments. Ordinary descendants, static initializers, and instance defaults exposed by static bodies stay within this same traversal. Static dispatch does not receive a third independent allowance, and exhaustion can leave its additional support incomplete.

The warning text identifies which allowance was exhausted: `Generic member discovery reached` refers to the primary allowance, while `Supplemental generic discovery reached` refers to the additive allowance. Both use `CSWINRTINTEROPGEN0106`, and both are promoted to errors when warnings-as-errors is enabled. Supplemental exhaustion can leave additional dispatch-derived support incomplete, but cannot displace signatures or member scans from primary discovery.

When receiver arguments and dispatch calls are discovered in different input modules, their late join runs in one additional bounded supplemental enumeration per result kind; it does not resume or reset either of the originating module's worklists. Pairs published before receiver-driven default discovery and their descendants drain first; pairs involving its new observations use the remaining allowance afterward. Imported receivers do not repeat their default scans. Generic and SZ-array discovery retain separate enumerations. The allowance is therefore 1024 primary plus 1024 supplemental reservations per module enumeration, plus the existing bounded late join, not a single application-wide limit.

Static-phase publications are admitted last, after the earlier join stages and their join-local instance-default fixpoint. They can join earlier receiver or demand observations without retrying already completed module-local pairs. The late counter is never reset between these stages.

Explicit signatures in the input assembly are discovery roots and do not count toward this limit. After the limit is reached, additional types found through member traversal can still be discovered, but their members are not scanned. Other discovery branches continue, and types only reachable through unscanned members may not receive all required marshalling support.

Simplify the expanding generic pattern if this warning affects a required interop path. Setting `CsWinRTGeneratorTreatWarningsAsErrors` to `true` (or passing `--treat-warnings-as-errors true` to the tool) makes reaching the limit fail generation.
